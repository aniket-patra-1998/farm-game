Barnlight privacy policy

Last updated: 27 November 2026

Barnlight is a farming game made by Aniket Patra ("we"). This page explains what happens to your information when you play.

No account

Barnlight has no sign-in and asks for no name, email or contact details. Your farm, progress and settings are saved on your phone. Uninstalling the game deletes them.

Ads

Barnlight offers optional rewarded ads: short videos you choose to watch for a small in-game reward. They are served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address and basic device and usage information, as described in Google's policy. Where the law requires it (for example in the EU and the UK), the game asks for your consent first, and you can change your choice later. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings.

Purchases

Gem packs and the starter pack are bought through Google Play. Google handles the payment; we receive only a confirmation that the purchase was made, never your payment details.

Play statistics

To improve the game, Barnlight sends anonymous play statistics (for example: levels reached, items bought, how long a session lasts) to our analytics provider, GameAnalytics. They are tied to a random ID made by the game, not to you, your advertising ID or your phone number. You can turn them off in Settings → Share play statistics.

The internet connection

Barnlight downloads its own pictures and models from our file host, Cloudflare. Like any website, Cloudflare receives your IP address and basic request details to deliver the files, under its own policy. We do not receive or store this information.

Notifications

If you allow them, the game sets reminders on your phone (for example, when crops are ready). They are scheduled on your phone; nothing is sent to us.

What we do not do

We do not sell your information, and we do not share it with anyone except the providers named above, for the purposes named above.

Children

Barnlight is meant for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children. Ads are limited to content suitable for teens.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.

Contact

Questions: write to the developer email shown on Barnlight's Google Play page.
