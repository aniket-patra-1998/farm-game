# Architecture: where everything is and how it fits

The detailed map of the code, moved out of CLAUDE.md so a session reads only what it touches. CLAUDE.md keeps
the hard rules and a short map; open the part of this file for the area you change.

## Layout
- `autoload/game.gd` (`Game`, class `GameState`): the shared core (coins, gems, XP, time, plots and crops,
  placed objects, mastery, tutorial), saving, and every signal. It owns one of each subsystem.
- One event stream (`Game.record(verb, subject, n)`, data/events.json): everything done on the farm (plow, plant, harvest, collect, buy, craft...) is recorded once, with what it was done to; it keeps the ribbon counters (each verb's rules pick its counter, by the subject's kind where one verb feeds two) and is told as `Game.did`, which goals listen to. Never bump a ribbon counter directly; a new action records its verb, and `tests/suites/events_test.gd` holds the code's verbs to the data's.
- `autoload/feedback.gd` (`Feedback`, data/feedback.json): the game's polish in one place: each moment's sound and buzz (`Feedback.moment("place")`) and the colour of each floating text (`Feedback.color("coins")`). Never call `Sound.play`/`Sound.buzz` for a game moment or hard-code a buzz length or float colour; add it to the data, and a test checks every name exists.
- `autoload/sound.gd` (`Sound`): sound effects and vibration. Every sound is a recipe in `data/sounds.json`
  (synthesised once, ahead of time) or a recording, or takes of one (`files`: a different take each time); code plays them by name (`Sound.play("harvest")`), and a
  test checks every name it asks for exists. Recordings are CC0 only, cut by tools/sound_cuts/make_sounds.py; what no traceable recording covers (horse, goose, the daytime farmyards, the music) is made from scratch by make_generated.py; every file in art/sound has its row in art/sound/SOURCES.md (Freesound page, uploader, CC0, or made here), and a test fails on one without. Animals call on a tap (`Sound.voice(family, young, id)`: a baby's own call or the adult's higher, `voice_pitch` per family or item, once per `VOICE_GAP_MS`). The farm's background sound and music are `Soundscape` (data/soundscape.json: a looping bed per part of the day, chosen by the day, kept to seasons; quieter under rain, storm, snow and fog, the rain itself being WeatherEvents' loop of the CC0 recording; music with long rests), on the Ambience and Music buses with their own volumes. Volume, mute and vibration live in `user://settings.cfg`, read and written only through `PlayerSettings` (graphics, the window and the debug readout keep their sections there too).
- Vehicles at work are a simulation in `scripts/systems/`, not a picture: `VehicleWork` drives the lanes of `VehicleRoute` with `VehicleMotion`'s weight (data/vehicle_motion.json; size and weight per vehicle in the catalog) and works each plot as it passes, run by `Game.vehicles.advance` (`settle()` for tests); the view only follows it.
- Layout synergies (`AdjacencySystem`, `Game.adjacency`, data/synergies.json; our own touch, Balance `synergy.enabled`): homes of one kind that share an edge pay +10% coins each, up to 4; derived like `LinksSystem`, nothing saved; shown as a line in the building panel and a glow and "+N%" tag while placing.
- Neighbours (`SocialSystem`, `Game.social`, data/social.json, Balance `social.*`; our own touch: an inbox where FarmVille had the feed): the inbox holds gifts, answers, shares and requests as plain `InboxItem` dictionaries, the outbox your own requests (a frame's materials, fuel), seedling shares and gifts. Free things are claimed; helping pays and counts against the day's helps; `help_all` does both. A share is additive (a copy is minted, the sharer keeps theirs). Who the neighbours are is a `NeighbourTransport` (`SimulatedNeighbours`: seeded per slot, so the same time always gives the same mail; real friends would be another class); `settle()` catches time up (only ever forward), never per frame (the inbox opening, and every few minutes for its count). Saves inbox, outbox and the day's limits. The one inbox (`HudInbox`, the Neighbours button and faces, with a count) is also FarmVille's Gift Box: things to place (`InboxItem.ITEM`) from neighbours or from the farm itself (`FARM`: a baby grown up, `SocialSystem.deliver`); Place claims it into the Barn and straight into your hand.
- Building rules (`BuildingSystem`, `Game.buildings`): every "can I?" about a building answers with a `Verdict` (yes, or a `Why` and the message): `can_buy`, `can_place` (`place_why` is the reason alone, for scans and per-frame checks; `Game.can_place` asks it), `can_move`, `can_move_in`, `can_store`, `can_finish`, `can_expand`, `can_rotate`, `can_sell`. The UI asks and shows the reason; it never repeats a rule. Every building's sheet opens with its picture, state and Move/Turn/Sell (`BuildingSheet`); `is_building_item` is "any building", `is_home` a building that holds things.
- Animal homes (the building-look design, data/building_models.json): each its own building with upgrades you can count, a second storey at the top sizes, a smaller building in a bigger yard. Heads at a home's windows (half full and up) are cut from each animal's file at its own size (`BuildingFill._head_mesh`), none when they don't fit. Every tapped building (homes, crafting buildings, the Barn, building sites, the rest), and an animal to feed, a young one, a bloom and a finished vehicle, opens as the one sheet at the bottom and everything it does is in it, never another window (`BuildingSheet`, built once and refilled; its middle and big button by `BuildingSheet.Kind`): tabs (`SheetTabs`) whose pages the panel builders fill and scroll (a home's Feed, Grow and its own extras by name, `SPECIAL_TABS`; the Barn's Storage, Boosts and Fuel; a young one's, a bloom's and a vehicle's own page), a crafting building's menu (`HudCrafting.tabs`: Make, Goods, Pantry); the builders ask for a page to be built again through `FarmHud.refresh_panels`; it drags between compact and tall (`TALL_SHARE`) and down to close, and `FarmHud.open_full_panel` is the sheet, tall; `tests/suites/menus_test.gd` taps every building and sweeps every menu on three screen shapes for broken words, raw ids and spills. Panel text (`BookPanel.text`, a `PanelText`) wraps only where it has a width, never a letter a line. A card floating free on the HUD (not in a container: the sheet, the away card) keeps its size through `UiKit.keep_fitted`, since Godot never shrinks such a Control; its Fill offers `HousingSystem.room_for_loose`, what would really move in.
- Building looks: each building has one signature feature no other has (`BuildingModel.SIGNATURES`: a goat's rock, the Spa's steaming pool, the gophers' burrow hill...; our own touch, beyond FarmVille's models), a test holds every pair of buildings apart by shape from the camera; the starter Barn looks broken until repaired (`_broken`); everything with something to collect (buildings, trees, blooms, animals) wears the one ready sign, FarmVille's soft pink triangle bobbing above it (`ReadyMarks`, one MultiMesh for all; `PlaceholderArt.draw_ready_marker` in the 2D world); crops show ripeness by their look. A building that can still grow shows what it lacks (our own touch, a rule for every building): `BuildingModel._plan` gathers each shape its next size has and this one lacks (told apart by place and size, so a repaint is no change) into one part, `when_plan_true`, drawn as a timber frame with the kit's `kit_frame` material (shaders/frame3d.gdshader: one draw, no shadow); it shows only on a building standing on the farm (`Objects3D.state_of`, Release `plans`), never in a shop card or on a broken Barn, and `"plan": false` in a building's description turns it off. A building that has just grown gets fireworks over its roof (`Fireworks3D`, one draw for every spark; `Objects3D.fireworks_over` from `Game.building_upgraded`, and for the Barn from `Game.vip_level_reached` too): one small golden burst, or for a building whose def says `grand()` (the Barn) three rockets bursting in gold, red and blue; nothing else moves for it.
- VIP (`VipSystem`, `Game.vip`, Balance `vip.*`, Release `vip`; our own touch, docs/VIP.md): a paid layer on top of the game, gating nothing. A VIP level (1 to 15, kept for good) comes from points: the daily gift's login streak (10 to 50 a day) or gems (10 points each). Bonuses work only while VIP is active (3 days with each new level, 7 more for gems): `VipSystem.boost` (1 to 2) multiplies the home boosts, the layout bonus and, through `VipSystem.chance`, the chances on a collection; every new bonus or chance a building gives goes through one of the two. The Barn shows it: its look is the VIP level (`BarnDef.look_level`; `Game.look_level`/`look_levels` are what a building's looks go by, not its size), with one big round badge (`VipBadge`: a coin with a crown and the level's number large on it, its colours by tier) that is two looks of one part (`when_vip_true` glimmers with the kit's `kit_shine` material, twinkles with a `sparkle_*` effect and turns slowly on a `spin_*` part, its number on both faces; `when_vip_false` is the same badge still and grey). The Barn is the biggest building (4 by 4 tiles) and its yard keeps company that grows with every five VIP levels (`fill.company`, `from` a look: two trees, then a cow and a pig from VIP 6, a tractor from VIP 11, each at its own spot, for show only), and its sheet has the VIP page (`HudBarn.build_vip`), which shows the chances with and without VIP.
- Vehicle rust (`RustSystem`, `Game.rust`, Balance `rust.*`, Release `rust`; our own touch, docs/RUST.md): a vehicle gathers a level of rust every 14 days parked in a Garage and every 7 standing outside, worked out from the clock (what is kept is the rust banked when it last changed places or was repaired; `settle` is told by both object signals, so a rule that moves a vehicle must announce it as every rule does). Each level burns 10% more fuel a plot and works 20% fewer plots (`VehicleWork`); at level 5 it does not work; a repair costs coins and time by level and takes it all off. With rust on, any vehicle owned can be driven (`usable_vehicles`), parked or not; upgrades still want it parked (`parked_vehicles`); after a job one standing outside goes into a Garage with room (`VehicleSystem.park`). Its rust shows in its paint (`VehicleModel.build`'s `rust`). A rusty vehicle wears the rust sign (`RustMarks`, one draw for all: a broken gear with a pip a level; over the vehicle outside, or over its Garage for the rustiest inside); a tap on the sign offers only its repair in a bubble (`FarmGrid.tapped_rust`, `HudBubble.show_repair`).
- Boosts per home (`HomeBoostSystem`, `Game.boosts`, data/home_boosts.json; our own touch, Balance `synergy.home_boosts`): a stocked home adds to a farm-wide boost (feed chance, golden luck), capped; derived, nothing saved.
- Pick up and drop (`Placement`, `HousingSystem.fit`/`homes_for`): what is carried is offered only to buildings that take it now (finished, right kind, room), lit by an outline of the building itself (`Objects3D.outline_of`, `grid.outliner`); unbuilt, broken or full ones are not marked and a drop on them (or on bad ground) sends a lifted or Barn-stored thing back where it came from, while a purchase stays in hand. Rules come from the catalog (`holds`, `holds_kind`); `tests/suites/drops_test.gd` checks every home from the data.
- New things arrive one way (`BarnStorage.welcome`, with `keep` for a thing's own fields): a baby born at a home, a nursery adult, a Mystery young found, a prize won, a breed traded for goes to the Barn while it is repaired and has room, else the one inbox (from the farm); never dropped on the farm or into the home it came from. The Barn never holds more than its own space: storing into a full Barn sends the thing to the inbox (`_send_on`), and an older save's overflow moves there on load (`spill_over`).
- Raising (`RaisingSystem`, `Game.raising`, Balance `raising.*`; FarmVille's way): babies in a nursery grow on Animal Feed, not harvests. Its trough (`trough` on the object) takes `feed_per_baby` per baby still growing (8 babies: 80), poured from the Animal Feed supply (NurtureSystem); each time it holds enough, the oldest (`growing`, arrival order) grows up and its adult arrives like any new thing (the Barn, else the inbox) to be placed. Feed comes from harvesting the nursery and animal homes, the Feed Mill's Animal Feed Sacks, and gems.
- Feed Mill (our own touch): a crafting building whose `feed_mill_recipes` (data/catalog/recipes.json, field `feed`) make rich feed for the Barn instead of goods. Dogs: a find family in data/finds.json with a Dog House; a family's `helps` adds to another family's find chance.
- `scripts/systems/*.gd`: the subsystems, each a `GameSystem` with its own state and rules, reached as
  `Game.crafting`, `Game.vehicles`, `Game.bees`, `Game.strays`, `Game.collecting` (collect), `Game.shop` (buy, place, sell), `Game.helpers`... `Game.player` is the farmer: farm work is
  queued there and happens when the farmer has walked to it (the farm grid shows results from its
  `action_done` signal; tests drive it with `player.advance(seconds)`). Each saves its own fields
  (`reset`/`save_to`/`load_from`); saving and loading the farm itself is `Persistence` (`Game.persistence`, with `SaveCheck` mending damaged saves); a new feature with its own state gets its own system, not more `game.gd`.
- The showcase farm (`ShowcaseFarm`, debug builds): the farm with everything on it, laid out from the catalog,
  then dressed by every system (`GameSystem.showcase()`): VIP at its top level and active, so the Barn stands
  as the farm's hero; vehicles at every stage of rust, half left outside the Garage; crafting buildings at
  work, finished and idle. What stands at one size or level at a time is walked from its card in Settings:
  every building a size down or up (each look, and the frame of what the next adds), the VIP level down or
  up, VIP lapsed or active. A new system with something to show implements `showcase()`.
- `data/catalog/*.json` + `data/catalog/schema.json`: every item, by category -> family -> variant.
  Loaded into the classes in `scripts/catalog/` (ItemDef -> CropDef, PlaceableDef -> ProducerDef ->
  TreeDef/AnimalDef, BuildingDef -> BarnDef/CraftingDef, VehicleDef, DecorationDef, RecipeDef...).
- `scripts/farm_grid.gd`: farm geometry and input (swipe to farm, our own touch, Balance `input.swipe_farm`/`swipe_tools`: a one-finger drag that starts on a plot the tool would work (`would_act`) works every plot on its path, once each, only adding work, quietly (the first refusal said once ends it), and the view stays put; any other drag, or two fingers, moves the view; the open plot under a farming tool lights as it lands; `Placement` in `scripts/world/placement.gd` holds what is in hand and the drop rules); owns the world layers in `scripts/world/`
  (GroundLayer, ShadowLayer, ObjectLayer of FarmPiece nodes, OverlayLayer) and `scripts/fx/`.
- `scripts/world/space_fit.gd` (`SpaceFit`, game-wide, pure rules, tests/suites/space_fit_test.gd): fitting things into space. Every tile keeps a clear border (`TILE_MARGIN`; `fit_model` brings a model inside it, so neighbours never touch), and `pack` puts things (animals in a yard, anything with a radius) in a room clear of its edge, of what is taken and of each other, drawn smaller when crowded (down to `MIN_SIZE`, or the caller's `smallest`: a yard's animals hardly shrink, `BuildingFill.ANIMAL_SMALLEST`, so fewer are out), then only as many as fit. Use it for any placing of things inside a space, not a new per-feature layout. A tree's crown overhangs its tile by design (never shrink trees to fit); instead `Game.can_place` (strict) keeps trees and buildings off each other's edges, and `settle_objects` is not strict so no save moves.
- `scripts/world3d/instancer.gd` (`Instancer`, game-wide): anything that repeats is built once per look and shared: `copy_of` (own node, shared meshes: standalone trees, buildings; `variant(row,col)` picks one of `VARIANTS` looks so copies are not clones) or `parts_of` + `place` (MultiMesh batches for crowds like orchard trees; far zoom swaps them for cheap blobs, `BuildingFill._plant_lod`). Build new repeated things through it, not from scratch each time.
- Made ahead of time, in the game (res://baked, by CI's phone build; our own engineering: space traded for a smooth phone): every building at every level, every decoration and tree variant, the showcase's models and the woods (`ModelStore`, `Objects3D.make_every_look`), every sound (`Sound.bake_all`), every item picture at its largest (`ItemIcons.SHIPPED`, tools/bake_icons.tscn, shrunk to the screen when loaded). Animals are copies of one built per kind and size (`Animal3D._from_template`).
- Assets made as files (docs/ASSETS.md, docs/ART_BIBLE.md): `AssetRegistry` maps an item id to its file and budget (data/assets.json); `Objects3D._build` and `ItemIcons` ask it first and draw the code-built stand-in when it has none; plain materials become `shaders/master_solid.gdshader`; parts named `when_<state>_<value>` show only in that state (`Objects3D.state_of`); `tests/suites/assets_test.gd` checks every listed file.
- Animals made as files: `tools/animal_art` (Python, offline) exports `art/models/animals/<id>.glb` (mesh, colours, sizes brought toward a cow's as FarmVille drew them (`export.shown`), moves as blend shapes: walk, head, look, blink, tail, breath, jaw, the rabbit's hop, ear flick, wing flap, the turkey's display; the bee beats its wings and hovers); listed in data/assets.json, `Animal3D._build_file` draws it and `_pose_file` sets its blend shapes from the same pose values a built animal uses (all 73: every breed, baby and dog; re-export with `python3 -m animal_art export ../../art/models/animals "<names>"`).
- The farmer made as a file: `tools/animal_art` `farmer` (farmer.py; `python3 -m animal_art farmer ../../art/models/farmer`) exports `art/models/farmer/farmer_<a or b>_<hair style>.glb` (a boy and a girl, `look.body`; chosen in Settings, `PlayerSystem.set_kind`): one smooth skin with a drawn face, FarmVille's proportions, no colours of its own (each point's first UV says whose colour it wears: skin, hair, shirt, overalls, shoes), limbs moved by blend shapes (legs forward and back, arms back and two raises, blink). `Farmer3D` tints the mesh once for `Game.player.look` (`tinted`) and sets the moves from the same angles its code-built stand-in swings; hats are still built in code and set on the head.
- Standalone decorations are drawn in batches (`DecorLayer`, `Objects3D.decor`; our own engineering, as the LinkLayer does for fences and paths): each is an empty stand-in node in `Objects3D` (its place, its box for taps: meta `BOX`) and an instance in one MultiMesh per part of its look per `REGION` of the farm, so draws follow the number of kinds, not of things; `Instancer.pieces_of` takes a built model apart for it; dropping in, bouncing and being lifted are played on the instance (`pulse`, `set_hidden`); the grass and stones round a decoration are a few shared looks. Only the kinds in `Objects3D.VARIED_DECOR` (built differently by where they stand) keep a node and several looks.
- Built models kept on disk (`ModelStore`, our own engineering): `Instancer` (and the woods) asks it before building; a look is loaded from res://baked (the showcase's models, baked by CI: `tools/perf_probe.tscn -- --bake=<stamp>` before the phone build) or user://models (saved the first time it is built on the phone), keyed by the build's stamp; without a stamp (editor, tests) everything is built fresh. Loaded materials are matched to ones in use by their settings (`signature`).
- `scripts/world/farm_camera.gd`: eased zoom, momentum, elastic edges; `FarmMain.zoom_limits`: the closest zoom shows `CLOSEST_TILES` across on any farm, and you can pull back `FURTHEST_OUT` past the whole farm. `Display`: a computer opens maximised (project.godot's window mode), or full screen if chosen in Settings; a resize keeps the player's view.
- Graphics quality (`Quality`, data/quality.json): low, mid, high or ultra (computers only), chosen in Settings (phone default mid; until the player chooses, `AutoQuality` times the frame all session and steps down its `ladder` at once when frames stay slow: high, mid, mid capped at 30, low): shadows and their splits and reach, glow, 3D resolution scale, how soon models drop to their simpler levels (`ModelKit.bake` gives every big merged mesh levels of detail, `with_lods`), how often animals are posed, whether crops cast shadows, anti-aliasing (`msaa`, off on low) and the shadow map's size, the frame cap (`max_fps`: 30 on low, none on mid and up; AutoQuality may add a 30 cap), and whether the full-screen grade pass runs (`screen_grade`, high only: in 3D the grade's saturation, contrast and night lift are the frame's own adjustments, `Farm3D.set_screen_grade`, and the 3D fog is the haze). A new costly look should be a field there, read through `Quality.value` and re-applied on `Quality.notifier`.
- Plots change one at a time: `Farm3D._plots_changed` gathers the changed plots once a frame and `Plots3D.update`, `Crops3D.update` (per-plot buffer pieces, only the groups a plot left or joined laid out again) and `Ground3D.update_tiles` touch only those; -1 means the whole field.
- Item pictures (`ItemIcons`): every card's picture is the item's own model rendered off screen in a small studio (key, fill and rim lights), the cards on screen first, kept on disk in user://icons, keyed by `ArtVersion.of(id)` (data/art_versions.json, generated by tools/make_art_versions.py and checked in CI: a fingerprint of the item's file, or of the builders for code-built looks), so a change to any art redraws its pictures by itself; anything new made from the art keys itself the same way. Each is drawn twice, on black and on white, at `IconDevelop.SUPER` size, and `IconDevelop` cuts it out from the two a few rows a frame (true see-through edges on any GPU, never a grey square); then `IconStyle` draws it once through shaders/icon_flat.gdshader into the pictures' flat sticker look (our own look: two tones of each colour's own hue, a line where tones meet, a dark outline and a white edge, no shadow) before it is shown and kept. A crop is pictured as its harvest (`CropForms.harvest`: a few pieces of its ripe produce, heavy ones heaped and upright ones held up as a bunch, no soil), keyed on the crop builders' code.
- Interface motion (`UiFx`, our own touch): buttons and cards press in and spring back (`press_bounce`), a panel swells open and its cards pop in one after another (`pop_in`, only as it opens). New buttons and cards use them. Panels are `BookPanel` on the kit: `open_panel` or `open_tabs`; a section inside a panel is flat on the paper with a thin line under it (`BookPanel.card_frame`), not a box in a box; each tab's page is built once while open and a page marked live (`BookPanel.set_live`) is updated in place on a refresh, not rebuilt. A menu of item cards is a `CardList` (scripts/ui/card_list.gd; our own engineering, for any number of items; the Market, seeds, Barn, Collections and Ribbons as cards, the inbox and recipes as `CardList.rows`, flat with a thin line between): a virtual grid where only the cards in view exist, light `CardFace`s (one node that draws itself: no box at rest, a soft highlight while pressed, the price a coin and a bold number) reused as it scrolls, a face (`face_of`) and picture worked out only for a card on screen, its id list worked out once at load; `tests/suites/hud_test.gd` holds 5,000 items to a budget. New card menus use it.
- Icons (`UiIcon`, `tools/icon_sheet.tscn` shows them all): the HUD's icons and the level star are vector files, art/icons/ui/<kind>.svg, made by tools/make_ui_icons.py (our own look: each part a soft top-lit gradient, an ink outline, a white sticker edge, glossy highlights), read by `UiSvg` (importer "keep") and drawn crisp at the size shown; a new HUD icon is a new entry there. Kinds with no file fall back to the 3D-rendered picture (`UiModels`), then to the drawn flat shape (`_shape`). Tools and menu buttons are icons only; names show on hover or a long press. The helpers' flower (`HudHelpers`, bottom left; our own touch): a wheelbarrow of supplies that blooms into Fertilize All, Unwither, farmhands, arborists, gardeners and fuel, each with its count. HUD buttons float over the farm with no box or strip behind them (`UiKit._float_states`): a soft paper disc under a finger, a fuller one ringed green round the tool in hand; their words are `FloatText`'s. Contents rows (`UiKit.count_row`: picture, count, name) and mastery stars (`MasteryStars`) are kit pieces, used wherever things are listed. Object icons (coin, gem, hoe, sacks, barn, market...: `UiModels.KINDS`) are small 3D models rendered by `ItemIcons` as "ui:<kind>" in the same flat sticker look as item pictures; symbols (eye, zoom, arrows) stay drawn. Tool and menu buttons are icon-only once known: a tool's name shows until picked `HudTools.LEARN_USES` times, and a long press on any button names it (`UiKit.long_press_name`).
- The interface is drawn at `UiTheme.scale()` (a tool button a set share of the screen's shorter side, never under a comfortable touch on a phone; data/ui_theme.json `scale`): smaller, rounder buttons than the design sizes; sizes named `*_share` aren't scaled; panel, popup and strip text is sized once through `UiTheme.font` (design px × scale × `text_share`, never under `MIN_FONT`). Performance has a budget: data/perf_budget.json, the showcase farm's draws and triangles on mid (`Objects3D.draw_cost`), held by a test; animals cast real shadows only on high and up (`animal_shadows`).
- The interface stays inside the screen's safe area (`Display.safe_insets`: notch, camera hole, gesture bar; the HUD root moves in), and item pictures are made at the screen's density (`ItemIcons.side`). In 3D the 2D night glow is off (windows, lamps and fireflies light themselves).
- Type: Poppins (fonts/, OFL), Medium the project's font for all text (project.godot `gui/theme/custom_font`), Bold (`UiTheme.bold`, its digits made one width by tools/make_tabular_font.py so changing numbers don't shift) for numbers, prices and floating words; never Godot's built-in font. Every size snaps to the type scale (`UiTheme.TYPE_SCALE`: caption 12, body 15, heading 18, title 24, display 32, through `UiTheme.font`). Numbers are written one way, by `UiTheme.number` (in full below 1,000, then short and rounded down: "4.8k", "125k", "1.2M"; data/ui_theme.json `numbers`) and `signed` ("+4.8k"); `exact` ("12,450") only where the exact figure matters; every place that shows words (messages, the bubble, floating words, panel text, cards, buttons) passes them through `UiTheme.tidy`, so a message's numbers come out the same, and `menus_test` fails on a number written long. Text sizes are roles (`UiTheme.CAPTION/BODY/HEADING/TITLE/DISPLAY`): panel words are body, `BookPanel.heading` a section's heading, only a title (meta `title`) larger; `menus_test` fails on bigger. Panels are `BookPanel.Shape.CARDS` (wide) or `LIST` (a column: Settings, the inbox); a setting is a `BookPanel.row` (name left, control right: `switch`, `choice` pills, a slider or field; what it does on a long press); a count over a bar is one `UiKit.word_bar`; details beyond a thing's one line sit behind an ⓘ (`BookPanel.more_button`, `BookPanel.info`), not printed; a sheet says each fact once (the ring the time, the big button only when there is something to do); times by `FarmGrid.format_duration` ("2h 15m"). Labels are sentence case; item names keep theirs.
- Weather's moments (docs/WEATHER.md, data/weather.json; our own touch): `WeatherEvents` (Main) strikes lightning in a storm as often as the data says (`struck(far, toward)`), plays thunder after the delay its distance gives (`thunder_near/mid/far`, recipes marked `long`) and the rain's loop as loud as the rain; `Lightning3D` draws the bolt past the farm's edge in view (never on it) and its `flash` lights the frame; fog is depth fog that begins past the farm (`Farm3D.fog_reach`), so the farm stays clear.
- Search (docs/SEARCH.md, data/search.json; our own touch): every `CardList` that holds `from_items` things puts a `SearchBar` over itself (instant, word starts first, plurals as one, one typo, found by what a thing is, gives and holds: `ItemSearch.words_of`; chips per menu; recent searches; "did you mean"); `SearchIndex` is the pure, budgeted engine. A new list is searchable with no work; name its `search_menu`, and give rows `search_words`.
- One look, paper (docs/UI.md "The unified look"): every panel, sheet and floating card is the same paper surface (`UiKit.paper_box`, `UiKit.paper_theme`), ink words by role (`BookPanel.INK/SOFT/HEAD/GOOD`), the title at the left and `UiKit.close_button`, three kinds of button (`UiKit.primary`, the cream panel button, `UiKit.quiet_button`), pill tabs; `tests/suites/menus_test.gd` fails on faint words or a wooden box inside a panel. New UI uses these, never colours of its own.
- Floating words (`FloatText`, our own look): everything the game says over the farm (the tap bubble, messages, reasons, the placing hint, the tutorial line) is bold white text with a dark outline and no box, rising in; things to do beside it are small round icon buttons (`FloatText.action`, named on a long press). Use it for any new words over the farm.
- Tapping a thing that isn't ready tells about it in a bubble above it, FarmVille's way, not a panel (`FarmGrid.tapped_info`, `FarmGrid.status_of`, `HudBubble`): its name and one line (ready in, withered, needs, being built); a thing with more to it (`HudBubble.has_panel`) offers Open for its panel. A vehicle with fuel still acts at once. Every other word about a tap (`FarmGrid.info`: can't sell that, plow first, not enough coins, doesn't fit) shows in the same bubble where the finger was (`FarmGrid.last_tap_at`); the game's own words within `FarmMain.TAP_WORDS_MS` of a tap (a refusal, a result) show there too; later news and anything said with a panel open stay a message at the top. The one thing to do right there (feed a hungry animal or home with the best feed in the Barn: `HudBubble.action_of`) is the bubble's own button; animals and trees have no panel.
- `scripts/hud.gd`: the heads-up display on the kit (`scripts/ui/`, data/ui_theme.json; docs/UI.md): `HudTop` (coins, cash, farm plaque, XP star), `HudTools` (seven tools; Select in hand by default, a press and hold lifts anything to move it), `HudSide` (the menu, Next ready and the whole-farm view; sound is in Settings, the interface rests on an idle farm, pinching zooms), messages and the placing bar; its panels live in `scripts/hud/` (`HudMarket`, `HudSeeds`, `HudCrafting`, `HudBuildings`, `HudBarn` (with the boosts), `HudBooks`, `HudSettings`, each reached as `hud.market` etc.). `scripts/placeholder_art.gd`: code-drawn art until painted art
  (`art/final`, via `SpriteBank`) replaces it.
- `tests/suites/*_test.gd`: rule tests, one `TestSuite` per area (helpers in `tests/test_suite.gd`), run by
  `tests/test_runner.gd` headless in CI. `tests/shots.gd`: screenshots, drawn by the Screenshots workflow (pushes to main, or on demand; apart from Tests, which the review waits on).
- Tools to look before and after a change: `tools/perf_probe.tscn` (frame cost, draw calls, what each system
  costs, on the showcase farm), `tools/contact_sheet.tscn` (every model in a labelled grid, to spot broken
  pieces) and `tools/state_sheet.tscn` (every building as a frame, empty, half full and full and ready); all
  run with a renderer under xvfb (see their headers).
- `scripts/world/animals/`: code-drawn, procedurally animated animals: `AnimalRig` (data/animal_rigs.json),
  `AnimalBehaviour` (what each animal does), `AnimalPose` (one frame), `AnimalArt` and its shape classes.
- `scripts/world3d/`: the farm in real 3D (`Farm3D`; the 2D world stays as a fallback). Models are built in
  code from data and merged by `ModelKit.bake`: `BuildingModel` (data/building_models.json: each size's
  changes, staged construction sites), `TreeModel` (data/tree_shapes.json), `VehicleModel`, `DecorModel`,
  `PondModel` (every pond: an uneven shore, a bank sloping into a bowl, water clear over the shallows via water3d `shore_fade`), `CropModels` + `CropForms` (data/crop_forms.json: a form per crop); `Animal3D` with `AnimalMotion` (springs: lean, sway, lag, squash, trot; every animal's contact shade is a slot in one `ContactShades` batch); `ModelKit.bake` merges painted parts of every finish into `coat()` and walls of every pattern into `wall_coat()` (what set them apart rides in the vertex alpha), so a building is a few draws; `BuildingFill`
  (animals out in yards, heads at the windows when full, produce piling up, doors opening on collect); `Ground3D` (shade and wear map, tufts; the ground's still look is painted once into a texture by `bake`, an unshaded top-down render of shaders/ground_bake (the shared look lives in ground_common.gdshaderinc), so a pixel costs one lookup plus a grain, and rain and snow maths run only when wet or snowy; tufts are kept per tile); `SurfaceWeather` (rain wets and snow settles on everything, eased over time, through the global shader values `wetness` and `snow_cover` (every global is set through `ShaderGlobals`, which remembers it; a test forbids direct writes) that the ground, wall, paint, canopy and foliage shaders read: a new surface shader should read them too); `LinkLayer` (fences, hedges, walls and paths join their neighbours: `LinksSystem` works out the sides from the farm, nothing is saved; `LinkShape` turns a 4-bit mask into one of six shapes, `LinkModels` builds each as a mesh shared by all pieces, the layer draws them as MultiMeshes and snaps what joined; groups in `data/linking.json`, keyed by catalog `shape`); `VehicleRuns`/`VehicleRun` (the picture of a vehicle at work: wheels and reels turning, springs, `DustPuffs`, tracks; it only follows the real run, `VehicleWork`; implements (plough, seed drill; meta `VehicleModel.LIFT`) go down to work and lift to drive, and the engine hums by `Feedback.hum("engine")`, a `"loop": true` recipe played by `Sound.start_loop`, capped at `Sound.MAX_LOOPS`); `Crowding` (a universal look: kinds listed in `Crowding.GROUPS` that stand packed together grow taller, wider and darker underneath, so a grove or orchard reads as one leafy mass over visible trunks; add a kind there and honour `crowd` in its model).

## The full rules (as they stood; CLAUDE.md keeps them short)
- Art follows docs/ASSETS.md: looks and smoothness first (size is no constraint up to 250 MB installed); art is made offline as files (glTF), imported under rules, asked for by name through one registry, with a fallback and a budget; code never builds art at runtime in new work.
- Run `tools/precheck.sh <godot>` before every push: what CI checks (gdlint's limits, generated docs, strings, all tests and the log gate).
- CI runs Tests on pushes to main and on pull requests, not on other branch pushes: a branch gets CI by opening a (draft) PR. Docs-only changes skip Tests, Screenshots and the phone build.
- A plot index's row and column are `GameState.row_of`/`col_of`, and `Game.on_farm(i)` says whether it lies on the farm now; never the maths by hand.
- docs/V1.md is the line for the first release (what is in, what is switched off, the dates and the launch bar); work outside it waits unless asked. `Release` (data/v1.json) applies it: `Release.has(id)` for anything sold, unlocked, given or found, `Release.on(feature)` for switched-off systems, and the release's own balance values; nothing is deleted, and tests run with everything on (`Release.whole`).
- docs/AUDIT.md is the plan: every system a full FarmVille replica first, smooth on low-end phones. A request
  is a starting point: apply each fix to every similar module, not just the one named.
- Follow `REVIEW.md`. Item behaviour on catalog classes; numbers as named constants or data.
- Player-facing text lives in `locale/strings.csv` (keys, en) and code shows it with `tr("KEY")`
  (`TranslationServer.translate` in static functions; constants hold keys). `tools/strings_lint.py` checks it in CI.
- Regenerate `docs/CATALOG.md`, `art/manifest.json`, `tools/generate_art.ipynb` and `data/art_versions.json` with their tools after
  catalog or art changes (CI checks them).
- Exported art stays tied to its builders: after changing a builder (tools/animal_art; the tree and crop builders and their data), re-export (tools/export_plants.tscn, `animal_art export` of every animal); each exporter records what it was made from in data/art_sources.json and CI fails when a builder changed since. Anything made from something else is keyed on what it is made from, never on a number bumped by hand.
- Every new farm action (feed, and any future one) is also offered to the helper that fits it (farmhands, arborists, vehicles), declared once in `data/actions.json` (`Actions`) and enforced by a test; never player-only by accident. Feeding (`FeedSystem`, data/feeds.json) is our own touch and switchable (`feeds.enabled`).
- Objects join and leave the farm only through `Game.add_object` / `remove_object` / `set_objects` (a test enforces it).
- A rule that changes one thing where it stands (feed, collect, fill, upgrade) says which: `Game.objects_updated(uid)`. It is told as `Game.object_changed(uid)` and the 3D world, the grid, the animals' minds and open panels look at that thing alone (`Objects3D.queue_one`); a move, purchase or sale (or a thing not standing on its tiles any more) is still the whole farm's `objects_changed`. `tests/suites/scale_test.gd` holds the fullest farm (every tile) and menus of 5,000 items to budgets.
