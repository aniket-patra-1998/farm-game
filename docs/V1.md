# Version 1

The line for the first release on Google Play: a small game that looks and feels finished. Everything built
beyond it stays in the code, switched off (`data/v1.json`), and comes back in updates.

## Dates

- **18 Nov 2026: Version 1 on Google Play's closed test track.** A personal developer account made after
  13 Nov 2023 must run a closed test with at least 12 testers, each opted in for 14 days in a row, before it
  can publish to everyone. The test starts the day the build is on the track.
- **During the 14 days**: the new art (animals, trees, crops) and fixes ship to the testers as updates.
- **About 3 Dec 2026: apply for production; public launch** after Google's review.

## In

| Area | Version 1 |
| --- | --- |
| Farming | plow, plant, harvest, wither, fertilise, mastery stars; about 20 crops |
| Animals | about 10 kinds (cow, chicken, pig, sheep, goat, horse, duck...) and their homes, collected from |
| Trees | about 10 fruit trees |
| Buildings | the Barn (storage), about 5 animal homes, 1 to 2 crafting buildings with a few recipes |
| Progress | coins, gems, XP and levels to about 20 with unlocks; the Market; farm expansion |
| Goals | the tutorial, then goals and daily tasks |
| Decorations | about 15, fences and hedges included |
| Neighbours | the inbox: simulated gifts and help requests |
| Feel | weather, day and night, sound and music, Settings |
| Saves | on the phone, and in the cloud through Google Play Games (a new phone gets the farm back) |
| Money | rewarded ads the player chooses to watch (finish a crop or building now, double a harvest, a daily bonus); no forced ads. Purchases: gem packs in three sizes and a one-time starter pack |
| Store | privacy policy, the Play data-safety form, the EU ads consent form, content rating, crash reports, the Android version Google requires |

## Off until updates

Vehicles; the nursery and raising; the Feed Mill; dogs and finds; strays; mystery young; bees; layout bonuses
and home boosts; collections and ribbons; the other crafting buildings; the rest of the animals, crops, trees,
buildings and decorations; the 2D world (removed: it would double every asset).

## Ready to launch when

- every item in version 1 is in the new art (docs/ART_BIBLE.md);
- 60 frames a second on a mid phone and 30 on a low one, held after 10 minutes; the farm opens in under 5 s;
- no crash and no lost save in the closed test; every test passes;
- purchases and ads work with test products and test ads, then live.
